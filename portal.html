04About

Navneet Singh, architect

I am an architect in New Delhi. I take a project from concept and drawings through BIM modelling, documentation and rendering to the final presentation.

When a task repeats, I write the tool for it: 58 Revit add-ins so far, a desktop app that turns a photo into a Revit family, an assistant for the open model, and an AI connector for Revit, Rhino, SketchUp, AutoCAD and Blender.

The Vasant Kunj house, from its design to its model and images, and every tool on this site are my own work.
