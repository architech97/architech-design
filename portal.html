I am an architect in New Delhi, in practice since 2021. I have worked on hospitality and data-centre projects, from 3D models and working drawings through BIM coordination to documentation, and I take my own projects from concept to rendering and the final presentation.

I also have strong expertise in Navisworks: multidisciplinary clash detection, model reviews, search sets, appearance profiles and Autodesk Construction Cloud issue workflows.

When a task repeats, I write the tool for it, in C# on the Revit API: {{revitTools}} Revit add-ins so far, {{desktopDetail}}, and {{connectorsPhrase}}, for Revit, Rhino, SketchUp, AutoCAD and Blender.

The Vasant Kunj house, from its design to its model and images, and every tool on this site are my own work.
